Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short: 3NDING has no accounts and does not collect your personal information on a server. What you read, save and write stays in your browser, on your device.

What is stored on your device

To work as a reader, 3NDING saves the following in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. This information is never sent to us.

  • Your favorites and which books you have marked as finished.
  • Your reading progress in each favorited book.
  • Your reader settings, such as font, theme, text size and voice speed.
  • Your daily reading time, daily goal and streak. Time is counted only while a chapter is open and you are active.
  • Passages you have highlighted.
  • The notebooks, chapters and drafts you write on the Write page.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not run advertising.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not read your highlights, notebooks or reading history.

Requests to other services

Using a website always involves some network requests. A few of them go to services other than 3NDING:

  • Book covers. Cover images may be loaded from an external image host. That host can see your IP address and basic browser details, as any website does when it serves an image.
  • Hosting. The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, time of request and the pages requested, for security and operations.
  • Read aloud. Speech is produced by your browser or operating system. Depending on your device, some voices may be processed by an online service run by your browser's vendor.
  • EPUB to TXT tool. Your EPUB file is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded. The page loads a script library from a public content delivery network and fonts from Google Fonts.

Sharing and backups

When you share a book link or a quote card, your device's own sharing options handle it, and you choose where it goes. Progress exports, Markdown exports and .zip bundles are created on your device and saved where you choose. 3NDING does not receive them.

Your choices and control

  • Use Export progress on the Library page to keep a backup, and Import progress to restore it on another device or browser.
  • Remove a book from favorites to clear its saved progress and highlights, or delete a notebook on the Write page.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for 3NDING permanently deletes everything described above. Because nothing is stored on a server, we cannot recover it for you.

Children

3NDING does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If we change how 3NDING handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Please check back from time to time.

See also our Terms of Use and the About page.
