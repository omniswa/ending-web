About 3NDING

3NDING is a quiet place to read. It is a small library you open in your browser, with no ads, no accounts and nothing standing between you and the page.

Read your way

Every book opens in a focused reader that you can shape to suit you. Choose a serif, sans or mono font, switch between Paper, Sepia, Dark and OLED themes, and adjust text size, line height and alignment. If your browser supports it, you can also have a chapter read aloud.

Keep your place

  • Add books to your favorites and 3NDING remembers the chapter and position where you left off.
  • Set a daily reading goal and build a streak, with a simple view of your last seven days.
  • Highlight passages as you read and turn them into shareable quote cards.

Write your own

The Write page is a notebook for books, stories or a personal journal. Organise your text into chapters, preview how it will read, and export it as a .zip or Markdown file. You can also import existing .txt, .md or .zip files.

Your data stays with you

3NDING has no accounts. Your favorites, progress, highlights and notebooks are saved in your own browser, and you can export a backup from the Library page at any time. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Tools

Got an EPUB you would like as plain text? The EPUB to TXT splitter breaks a book into one text file per chapter, entirely in your browser.
